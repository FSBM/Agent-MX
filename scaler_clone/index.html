Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Scaler Academy different?

Scaler Academy offers an industry-vetted curriculum, 1:1 mentorship from top tech professionals, and a strong focus on practical, problem-solving skills. Our programs are designed to accelerate your career growth significantly.

Who are the mentors at Scaler?

Our mentors are experienced software engineers and data scientists from leading tech companies like Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and Meta. They provide personalized guidance and insights.

What career support does Scaler provide?

We offer extensive career support, including resume building, interview preparation, mock interviews, and access to our network of hiring partners to help you land your dream job.

Are there prerequisites to join Scaler programs?

While some programs require basic programming knowledge, others are designed for beginners. We have an admissions process to assess your aptitude and guide you to the right program.
